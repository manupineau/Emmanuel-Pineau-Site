Out of Disorders

This ongoing series launched in 2018 features self-portraits and uncertain images. Disorders are sometimes the consequences of a state of mind or can be the consequence of it. I try to use it creatively by losing control of the photographic process through experiments with light and exposure. The result of this strange imagery composes a multiple and confusing portrait.
