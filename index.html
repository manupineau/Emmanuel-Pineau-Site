Emmanuel Pineau is based in Paris and has been working for several years in collaboration with photographer Sophie Delaporte on her projects in France and abroad, for fashion, beauty or advertising. Trained at the ENS Louis Lumière, he has a good experience in cinematography on movies, advertising or video art productions. His passion for analog photography makes it his favorite medium for personal work, with a pronounced taste for black and white square format.
